DKB

DKB (다크비) is a 8-member boy group under Brave Entertainment. The group consists of E-Chan, D1, GK, Heechan, Lune, Junseo, Yuku, and Harry June. They debuted on February 3, 2020 with ‘Sorry Mama’. DKB stands for "Dark Brown Eyes".Fandom Name: BB (DKB’s Bestie)
